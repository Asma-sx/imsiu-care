UX / HCI Project · Clickable Prototype

IMSIU Care

A mobile app that helps Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University students book campus clinic appointments in a few taps, without waiting in line or calling the clinic.

Design process

  1. 1User research and personas (students, faculty, clinic staff)
  2. 2Task analysis and scenarios for booking an appointment
  3. 3Studied similar apps, then defined functional and non-functional requirements
  4. 4Paper sketches, then a high-fidelity prototype
  5. 5Evaluation with a student questionnaire, then a redesign

Evaluation results

4.8/5Easy to navigate the app
4.7/5More comfortable managing health needs
3.4/5Easy to find the clinic, so we redesigned

Redesign based on feedback
